# Product Walkthrough

Use [Atrion](https://www.atrion.online/) with a normal account. Test credentials, when provided, are shared privately. Sign-in does not bypass registration.

## 1. Generate a building

Open Design Engine and enter:

> Create an eight-storey office building with a flat roof and large windows.

Inspect the whole building, orbit the camera and select a part. Enable section view only when you want to inspect the inside. Check the stated generation source and any warnings.

## 2. Plan an interior

Open Design and Interior and enter:

> A Scandinavian living room, 5 by 6 metres, with a sofa, two armchairs, a coffee table, a bookcase and a floor lamp.

Answer any clarification, then inspect the layout. Select an armchair, move or rotate it, delete it and undo the deletion. Compare the procurement list before and after editing.

## 3. Inspect a house and its plan

Describe a house with dimensions, floors and named rooms. Inspect its exterior, select a floor and review room furnishing, doors and windows.

Export the SVG floor plan and GLB. The house export contains all floors, even when only one is shown.

## 4. Explain the result

The editable workflow produces structured geometry and concept layouts. The optional realistic workflow produces a textured mesh through a separate inference service.

Generated concepts need review. Procurement is preliminary; demonstration prices are not supplier quotes. Unity Editor import has not been verified.
